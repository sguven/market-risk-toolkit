Market Risk Toolkit

Three self-contained educational laboratories on derivatives pricing and trading-book risk. Every formula is implemented in Python, tested against closed forms and published reference values, and tied to the Basel / U.S. rule text.

Option pricing

Black-Scholes Laboratory

Black-Scholes-Merton, Greeks and hedging, exotic options, Monte Carlo, FRTB-SA capital, VaR / ES, stress testing and SR 11-7 model validation.

Counterparty Exposure & XVA Laboratory

Exposure simulation, netting and CSA collateral, CVA / DVA / FVA / MVA / KVA, wrong-way risk, SA-CCR, IMM, BA-CVA and margin rules.

Open the lab → Second-order risks

Volatility & Correlation Risk Laboratory

Volatility estimation, the implied volatility surface, vega risk, variance swaps, correlation estimation, breakdown and stress, and how FRTB capitalises both.
